Virus in sewage, per province

How fast the virus load in sewage is rising in each province. Drag the slider or press play to step through every week since 2020.

The map is built from per-plant measurements, which load only when you ask for them.

Each plant counts toward a province in proportion to the residents it serves there (CBS, 1 January 2024). Growth is the trend over the four weeks up to the selected week (a straight-line fit through the log of each weekly level), shown as change per week. Hatching marks provinces where many residents live in areas whose plant had no measurement that week, so their latest value is more than a week old and does not count. Since July 2025 RIVM samples about 40% of plants each week, mostly the larger ones. The latest week can be incomplete, and RIVM revises recent values. Four Gelderland plants that closed in 2020–21 are not in the CBS table and are left out.

Data: Rijksinstituut voor Volksgezondheid en Milieu (RIVM), data.rivm.nl/covid-19, public domain. Also listed on data.overheid.nl; the "modified" date shown there is the date the dataset was first registered, not the date of the data.

The per-variant chart treats counts as nested: per RIVM's metadata, a sub-variant's cases are a subset of its parent's, so each parent keeps only the cases not assigned to a listed sub-variant. Samples not assigned to any listed variant count as "Other". Recent weeks sometimes list one sample under two sibling lineages, so shares are computed over all assignments in the month rather than over the sample count.
